Combat Quick Guide

  1. SetupPick units, place them on the grid. The blue "Start" button below the grid begins the battle.
  2. InitiativeThe top bar shows the turn order. The unit with the glowing "Acting" tag is up.
  3. Take ActionOn your turn, pick from Move / Attack / Defend / Skill / Item / End. Hover the grid to preview range.
  4. Dice & AssistOpen Battle Assist for Auto Turn / Auto Round, manual dice queue, and animation toggle.
  5. OutcomeWhen all of one team falls, you'll see Victory or Defeat with loot. Campaign battles return automatically.
