# Bazaar Story Summary

Read this first for Bazaar story work. The Bazaar of Broken Chains is the optional cross-world economy hub: arena tests, auction sinks, contracts, weird food, and trade consequences.

## Current Arc

Bazaar has no full authored sequence arc yet. The current playable slice is the Central Bazaar Ring travel map plus arena/auction activities. It should feel bright, strange, transactional, and funny, with danger hidden inside polite rules.
