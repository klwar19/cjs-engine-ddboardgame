# Map Art Prompts

Use these prompts for travel-map backdrop generation. The resulting images should be saved as game assets and then referenced from the matching `visualBackdrop` field in the travel-map JSON.

## Zombie Apocalypse Map

Create a 16:9 visual-novel travel map backdrop for a zombie apocalypse city called Last Light. Show a readable top-down/three-quarter city slice with a rooftop safehouse at left, ruined mall district near upper center, clinic block to the right, subway line across the lower half, and a broadcast tower at lower right. Mood: green dusk, rain-wet asphalt, broken signage, abandoned cars, barricades, overgrown cracks, distant smoke, practical survival horror. Keep the center areas clear enough for small UI markers and labels; no text, no logos, no characters, no gore, no heavy blur.

## Bazaar Map

Create a 16:9 visual-novel travel map backdrop for the Bazaar of Broken Chains, a cosmic inter-world market built around a bright central ring. Show a readable top-down/three-quarter bazaar slice with the Gate of Scales at left, Lantern Arena near upper center, Glass Gavel auction house at right, Lantern Food Row at lower left, and Prize Board Annex at lower right. Mood: warm lanterns, turquoise portal light, brass scales, hanging banners, floating stalls, broken-chain motifs, crowded but not cluttered. Leave clear open lanes for UI map markers and labels; no readable text, no logos, no main characters, no heavy blur.
